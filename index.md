to lead, with a manager section as an extra.

Every page has the same parts: **In one minute** (the spoken answer), **Key ideas**,
**On my CV**, **Likely questions**, **Pitfalls**, **Sources**.

Priority: **P1** asked in depth, **P2** likely once, **P3** a two-minute answer.
